## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Narrative
This design system embodies high-fashion African contemporary luxury paired with cutting-edge artificial intelligence. Rooted in West African textile heritage (wax hollandais, bazin riche, bogolan, kente), it merges ancestral craftsmanship with seamless, high-tech virtual try-on experiences. The aesthetic balances warmth, prestige, and digital agility:
- **Heritage & Sophistication:** Reverence for intricate textile patterns, structured silhouettes, and bespoke tailoring.
- **Innovation & Confidence:** AI interactions feel magical, frictionless, and reassuring, banishing uncertainty regarding garment fit and drape.
- **Warm Minimalist Editorial:** Spacious layouts that let vibrant fabrics speak, rejecting sensory clutter in favor of gallery-grade restraint.

### Aesthetic Movement: Contemporary Editorial Warm-Minimalism
The interface relies on warm ivory surfaces, delicate sand undertones, rich ebony contrast, and sun-drenched terracotta highlights. Glassmorphic overlays with warm blurs are reserved strictly for AI preview HUDs and interactive fitting bottom sheets, giving the technological elements a light, ethereal presence against traditional textiles.

## Colors

### Palette Philosophy
The palette draws direct inspiration from Sahelian sunsets, rich terracotta earth, deep West African foliage, and artisanal loom shuttles:
- **Primary (`#C85A32` - Terracotta Sienne):** The core call-to-action and primary brand accent. Evokes baked clay, sun-warmed earth, and vitality. Used for high-intent actions, primary buttons, active AI states, and highlighted tags.
- **Secondary (`#1B3B36` - Émeraude Forêt Profonde):** Provides grounding, high-fashion contrast. Used for structural badges, bespoke tailoring badges, selection borders, and premium guarantees.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A373` - Sable Doré):** A warm metallic sand accent for subtle iconography, ratings, luxury micro-accents, and AI scanning indicators.
- **Neutral (`#1A1A1A` - Ébène Intense):** High-contrast typography and icon color, delivering clear legibility against pale backdrops.

### Surface Architecture (Light Mode Default)
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Ivoire Coton), a soft, non-sterile off-white that honors raw fabrics.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards, Sheets):** `#FFFFFF` (Pur Lin) with warm undertones.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Tonal Containers):** `#F3EFEA` (Sable Clair) for chips, search bars, and subtle backplates.
- **Text & Contrast:** Primary text is `#1A1A1A`, secondary text is `#6E6B65`, and muted details sit at `#9E9A92`.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Display & Headlines:** `Playfair Display` establishes a high-fashion, lookbook aesthetic. Its high contrast stems from haute couture editorial conventions while conveying the craftsmanship of premium bazin and wax prints.
- **Body & Functional UI:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides crisp, friendly legibility across mobile devices, high ambient glare environments, and small-screen configurations. Its rounded, open apertures counterbalance the sharp serifs of the headings.

### Numerics & E-Commerce Pricing
All monetary notations (FCFA / XOF) use `Plus Jakarta Sans` in semi-bold and bold weights with tabular lining figures. This prevents visual jitter in product grids, checkout ledgers, and cart recalculations.

## Layout & Spacing

### Mobile-First Layout Model
The layout utilizes an 8pt architectural rhythm, optimized for fluid mobile screens (360px - 430px base viewport width):
- **Base Grid:** 4-column layout on mobile, transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and 12-column on desktop/tablet landscape.
- **Gutters & Safe Areas:** 16px (`1rem`) default mobile gutter and horizontal margins, expanding to 24px (`1.5rem`) on tablets. Dynamic margins respect OS safe areas at top status bars and bottom navigation bars.
- **Vertical Spacing:** Generous whitespace around high-resolution garments avoids visual tension between intricate textile patterns and transactional controls.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Warm-Shadow System
Visual depth is defined through warm-tinted ambient occlusion rather than cold digital drop shadows:
- **Base Shadow (Cards, Buttons):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.08)`, a warm, terracotta-tinted diffused shadow that grounds floating cards onto ivory surfaces without looking dirty.
- **Active State / Hover:** `0 8px 30px -4px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.12)`, offering crisp elevation during gestures.
- **Bottom Sheets (Virtual Fitting HUD):** `0 -10px 40px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.16)` with a frosted glass backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur: 16px; background: rgba(250, 248, 245, 0.88)`).

### Layer Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#FAF8F5` base.
- **Level 1 (Catalog Tiles & Cards):** Flat surface `#FFFFFF` with 1px border (`#ECE7DF`) and base warm shadow.
- **Level 2 (Persistent Navigation & Badges):** Sticky tab bars, bottom search anchors.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom-Sheets):** AI fitting interface, biometric/silhouette selector overlays.
- **Level 4 (Toasts & AI Notifications):** High-priority alerts hovering over interactive viewports.

## Shapes

### Form Language: Curated Fluidity
With roundedness level 2:
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Floating Badges):** 8px (`0.5rem`) to 16px (`1rem`) for harmonious interaction.
- **Cards & Product Containers:** 16px (`1rem`) corner radius to create framed, soft-cornered windows showcasing clothing textiles.
- **Bottom-Sheets & AI Viewports:** 24px (`1.5rem`) upper corner radius (`rounded-t-xl`) for tactile, sheet-pulling ergonomics.
- **Action Pills (Category Filters, Status Badges):** Fully rounded pill geometry (`9999px`) for quick thumb selection.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action / Add to Bag / Try On):** Background `#C85A32`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `12px` or pill (`9999px`), height `52px` on mobile for thumb ergonomics. Features a warm micro-shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(200, 90, 50, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Explore / Details):** Outline button with 1.5px border `#1B3B36`, text `#1B3B36`, transparent background, with active fill transitioning to `#1B3B36` and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **AI Fitting Button (Signature CTA):** Subtle linear gradient from `#C85A32` to `#E06D53`, accompanied by a polished star/sparkle icon in `#FAF8F5`, accompanied by an active subtle glow animation.

### Category Chips & Badges
- **Category Chips:** Horizontal carousel format, pill-shaped (`9999px`), padding `8px 16px`. Default state is background `#F3EFEA` with text `#1A1A1A`. Active state is background `#1B3B36` with text `#FAF8F5`.
- **Textile & Craft Badges:** Subtle square-pill tags (`radius 6px`) placed at the top-left of product cards: e.g., "Bazin Riche", "Wax Véritable", "Soie & Bogolan", tinted in pale sand `#F3EFEA` with text `#1B3B36`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Vertical 3:4 aspect ratio image container. Rounded corners (`16px`), 1px structural outline (`#ECE7DF`).
- **Floating Overlays:** Wishlist heart button positioned top-right with blurred circular backdrop (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)`). "AI Try-On" quick-trigger pill anchored bottom-right of the image thumbnail.
- **Pricing Notation:** Displayed prominently in bold: `45 000 FCFA` (with a space separating thousands for clear West African monetary conventions).

### AI Virtual Try-On Modal (Bottom-Sheet)
- **Behavior:** Slides up from viewport bottom, default height `85vh` with a soft handle pill (`40px x 4px`, color `#D4CFC9`).
- **Split Canvas:** Interactive upper viewport featuring the user's avatar or uploaded photo wearing the selected garment, powered by real-time rendering. Lower section displays fabric drape adjustments, size selectors (S, M, L, Sur-Mesure), and instant skin-tone harmonization indicators.
- **Texture Zoom:** Micro-zoom control enabling users to inspect the thread count and metallic sheen of gold-threaded bazin prints.

### Loading & Progress States
- **AI Processing State:** Clean pulsating ring combining Terracotta (`#C85A32`) and Sand Gold (`#D4A373`). Minimalist copy: *"Ajustement des textures et tombé du tissu..."*
- **Skeletons:** Warm shimmering gradient sweeping across `#F3EFEA` to `#FAF8F5`, preventing cold gray placeholders.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE7DF`, border radius `10px`. Focus state shifts to `1.5px solid #C85A32` with a subtle focus ring.
- **Measurement Selectors (Custom Tailoring):** Segmented buttons allowing fast switching between standard sizing and bespoke measurements (Tour de poitrine, tour de taille, longueur boubou).
